# Handover privacy statement

Handover is a Forge app built for the Atlassian Codegeist 2026 hackathon.

What it reads: Jira issues (summary, status, priority, assignee, reporter, components, labels, dates, comments), Jira user display names, Confluence page titles, labels, authors and body text, and Teamwork Graph relationships for the people in your site. All reads happen with the permissions of the signed-in user.

What it stores: on Atlassian Forge storage (inside Atlassian's infrastructure, scoped to your site) it keeps app settings, the latest analysis snapshot (account IDs, display names, per-area contribution shares), recorded absences you enter, a short history of risk scores and the list of handover pages it created. It does not store issue or page bodies.

What it writes: only when you press Publish or run the weekly report: Confluence pages and Jira follow-up tasks or comments.

External services: none. The app makes no calls outside Atlassian and sends no data to the developer or any third party.

Deletion: use "Clear stored data" in Handover > Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes its storage.
